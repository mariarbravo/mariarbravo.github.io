About me

I'm a Software Engineering student at the University of Arizona (GPA 3.84), graduating with my B.S. in May 2027 and continuing in the accelerated M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering through May 2028.

I'm passionate about software development and the different ways technology can be used to solve real-world problems. I enjoy working across the entire development process, from programming in C, C++, Java, and Python to building full-stack applications with Spring Boot, React, and PostgreSQL. My interests extend beyond traditional software development into artificial intelligence, computational engineering, and the intersection of software and physical systems.

I enjoy taking on new technical challenges, learning how complex systems work, and turning ideas into something functional.
